# Voice and I DO script (required)

Plans are written as **the teacher’s own notes**, in the same spirit as Khansa’s CH3 LP — not as instructions from an AI to a teacher.

## Voice rules

1. **First-person continuous** for teacher moves:
   - Prefer: *I am writing…*, *I am asking…*, *I am showing…*, *I am concluding…*
   - Section/action labels as gerunds where natural: **Writing:** / **Asking:** / **Saying:** / **Concluding:**
   - Avoid bare commands aimed at the teacher: ~~Write:~~ ~~Show:~~ ~~Emphasize:~~ ~~Model:~~ ~~Lead to:~~ ~~Circulate:~~ ~~Ensure:~~ ~~Remember to:~~

2. **No AI-instructor tone**
   - Ban phrases like: “Teacher should…”, “Make sure to…”, “Emphasize that…”, “Lead students to…”, “Probe…”, “Minimal talk”, “ONE method focus”, “Breakdown for word problem:”
   - Describe what *I* am doing and what *students* are saying/doing.

3. **Students’ lines are explicit**
   - Use `Ask:` / `Students:` / `Saying:` / `Writing:` blocks (same pattern as CH3).

4. **WE DO / YOU DO in the same voice**
   - WE DO: “I am giving pairs… Students are working on… I am moving around and helping when needed.”
   - YOU DO: state the exact question; “Students are solving this independently after GP.”

## I DO shape (match CH3)

Timed mini-sections with titles. Inside each section: show → ask → students answer → say/write → next ask.

```text
I DO:

2. INTRODUCE THE LETTER-NUMBER
5–6 minutes

I am writing on the board:

Shabnam’s age = Aftab’s age + 3

I am asking:

“Instead of writing ‘Aftab’s age’ every time, can we use a short letter?”

Students:

“Yes.”

I am saying:

“Let a stand for Aftab’s age and s for Shabnam’s age.”

I am writing:

s = a + 3

I am asking:

“If a is 23, what is s?”

Students:

“26.”

I am concluding:

“a and s are letter-numbers, and a + 3 is an algebraic expression.”
```

## Hook shape

Same voice — questions and expected student lines, not “Lead to:”.

```text
DO NOW (4 min)

Q1. …

SPARK – Age Story (5–6 min)

I am showing two name cards: Aftab and Shabnam.

I am asking:

“Every time Aftab’s age changes, how do we find Shabnam’s age?”

Students:

“We add 3.”

I am asking:

“Can we write one short rule that works for any age of Aftab?”

Students:

“We need something that can stand for any number.”
```

## Quick fail list (voice)

1. Imperative checklist I DO (`1. Write… 2. Introduce… 3. Emphasize…`)
2. “Teacher: circulate / minimal talk”
3. “Lead to:” / “Expected path:” as meta-instructions
4. Third-person coaching of the teacher
5. Missing Ask → Students pairs in I DO
