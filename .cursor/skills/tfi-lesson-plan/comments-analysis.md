## Themes (frequency → skill implication)

1. **Make GP/IP visible** (highest frequency) — plans often buried practice inside prose or omitted YOU DO questions.
2. **Fit 40 minutes / split outcomes** — ambitious flows and multi-method lessons overran.
3. **Align IP & Blooms skill to SWBAT** — practice and skill tags drifted from the stated outcome.
4. **Fill every column + surface sub-concepts in flow** — empty cells and “listed but not taught” micro-concepts.
5. **Pull CFUs into the CFU column** — checks lived only inside the narrative flow.
6. **Time-stamp every section + say when students solve** — missing minute budgets and practice timing.
7. **Recap / prerequisite refresh** — needed when prior topic was a struggle (conversions, mixed↔improper).
8. **Visual hooks; hands-on; one method; word problems in class** — reduce teacher-led abstraction.
9. **Calendar hygiene** — no weekend / no-class dates; revision rows need real plans.
10. **AI as enhancer, not author** — mentor accepts AI for sequencing language if content is teacher-owned.
11. **Reflections after class** — especially when timing was tight.
